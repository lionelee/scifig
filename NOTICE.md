# Third-party material

scifig-agents is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to
SciDraw AI (sci-draw.com), and it calls none of that service's interfaces.

## Bundled data

**`skills/scifig/references/journal-figure-requirements.json`**
An unmodified copy of "Journal figure requirements: size, resolution, format and font
specifications by publisher" by SciDraw AI (2026), https://sci-draw.com/journal-figure-requirements,
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Last verified by its
publisher on 2026-07-21.

**`skills/scifig/scripts/journals.py`**
Numeric presets hand-encoded from the data set above (a derived work under the same CC BY 4.0
terms for the encoded values). The underlying requirements belong to the respective publishers.

**`skills/scifig/references/ai-image-policies.md`**
A condensed table adapted from "AI-generated figure policies by journal and publisher" by
SciDraw AI (2026), https://sci-draw.com/ai-figure-policy, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Last verified
by its publisher on 2026-07-21. Rows were shortened; the policies belong to the respective publishers.

## Colour palettes

- Okabe-Ito colour-universal-design palette: Masataka Okabe and Kei Ito, https://jfly.uni-koeln.de/color/
- "Bright" and "muted" qualitative schemes: Paul Tol, https://personal.sron.nl/~pault/

## Methods

- Colour-vision-deficiency simulation matrices: G. M. Machado, M. M. Oliveira and L. A. F. Fernandes,
  "A physiologically-based model for simulation of color vision deficiency", IEEE TVCG 15(6), 2009.
- Arrow meanings in `skills/scifig/references/figure-recipes.md` are those of the Systems Biology
  Graphical Notation (SBGN) Process Description language, https://sbgn.github.io/specifications,
  as summarised in "Scientific diagram notation reference: SBGN arcs, standards and conventions"
  by SciDraw AI (2026), https://sci-draw.com/scientific-diagram-notation, CC BY 4.0, last verified
  by its publisher on 2026-07-21.
- The graphical-abstract dimensions quoted in the same file were read from Elsevier's
  graphical-abstract page on 2026-11-11.
